02 · Our approach

We clean like it’s
our own home.

Anyone can wipe a surface. A proper clean is a discipline — a sequence, a checklist and a standard you can feel the moment you walk in the door. This is the method we bring to every visit, whether it’s a studio apartment or a four-storey office.

  1. 01

    We walk through first

    Before a single cloth comes out, we walk the space with you — priorities, delicate items, entry notes and anything off-limits. Your home, your rules.

  2. 02

    A written checklist, every room

    Kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas — the same rigorous, room-by-room checklist on every visit. Ours, combined with yours.

  3. 03

    A final pass, together

    We finish with a walkthrough. If something isn’t right, we re-do it before we leave — not after you’ve messaged us on Friday night.
